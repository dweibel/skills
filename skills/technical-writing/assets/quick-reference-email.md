# Quick Reference: Email

Direct register. High signal, zero filler, one purpose per message.

## Structure

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Subject | Bracketed tag + specific topic: `[Action Required] Q3 Budget due Friday` |
| Opening | BLUF — purpose in the first sentence |
| Body | Short paragraphs (2–3 sentences max), bullet lists, bolded key facts |
| Closing | Explicit CTA: who, what, when |
| Sign-off | Plain. "Hi Jane," / your name. |

## Subject Line Tags

- `[Action Required]` — reader must do something
- `[Decision Needed]` — reader must choose
- `[Info]` or `[FYI]` — no action, awareness only

## BLUF Examples

- "I need your sign-off on the revised timeline by Thursday."
- "The payments team has decided to proceed with Option B."
- "CI is broken on main — here's what happened and what we're doing."

## Formatting for Skimmers

- Paragraphs: 2–3 sentences max.
- Three or more items of any kind become bullet points.
- Bold dates, deadlines, owner names, key decisions.
- Never bold entire paragraphs.

## Call to Action

Isolate the ask at the bottom, visually separated. Specify who, what, when:

> **@Dev Team:** Review the Figma files and flag blockers by **Thursday 5 PM**.

## When Email Becomes Memo

If the body grows beyond a few paragraphs (proposal, status update, post-mortem), switch to the memo template. The delivery channel doesn't change the structure.

## Voice Reminders

- Active voice throughout. Cut filler: just, actually, very, basically.
- Plain English: use not utilize, start not initiate, help not facilitate.
- No banned vocabulary. No em dashes.

# Quick Reference: Leadership Essay / Address to Your Own Team

Long-form structure. Measured first person. The audience is also the subject.

## Before Drafting

- Fill in `assets/style-sheet-template.md`: the document's name for itself, the name for your own set-apart sections, capitalization decisions for domain terms.
- Name the one specific reader you are writing to, and the group that reader belongs to.

## Structure

- Structure file applies in full: problem-first opening, one question per H2, distilled closing.
- Mark the boundary between borrowed material and your own voice visibly and consistently.
- Tables: every cell in a column answers the header's question in the same grammatical shape.

## Register

- First person is expected. State your position and your reasoning.
- Enthusiasm and alarm both read as pressure from a leader. Neither belongs.
- Paraphrases of a shared canon stay close enough to the source wording to be recognized.

## Revision, in This Order

1. Full checklist on the draft.
2. Reader-impact pass: every sentence that describes a group who will read this, read again from inside that group. Restate so they can act on it rather than absorb it as a verdict.
3. Connotation pass on every verb applied to the audience or to subjects they revere.
4. Style-sheet pass: self-reference, capitalization, chosen terms.
5. Re-check every paragraph you changed in steps 1 through 4 for banned vocabulary, fragments, and capitalization. Diff against the previous draft; do not rely on memory.

## Delivery

- Build the rendered artifact and read it. Lists need a blank line before them in Pandoc.
- Check the table of contents against the headings after the final edit.

## Voice Reminders

- Concrete subjects. Nominalizations back to verbs. Cut doublets.
- Active voice. Plain verbs. No banned vocabulary. No em dashes.
- Never credit an AI tool. No "produced with Claude Code assistance", no "generated with" footer, no model co-author trailer. Check datelines, signatures and footers.
- Vary sentence length. Compress overwrought parallelism (Hazard 19).

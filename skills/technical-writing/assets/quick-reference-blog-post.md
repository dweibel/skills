# Quick Reference: Short-Form Blog Post

Curiosity wins. Composure dialed back.

## Shape

- Open with the specific question, observation, or finding. No setup, no "in this post we will."
- Walk through the investigation in the order you moved through it. Failed attempts belong.
- Stop when the question is answered. A "Conclusion" section is rarely needed.

## Typical Arc

A small concrete thing → what surprised me → how I figured out what was happening → the mental model I came away with.

## Conventions

- First person is welcomed. "I noticed that…" or "I was trying to figure out…"
- Fragments are freely used.
- Single-question pieces are a complete form.
- TIL posts are legitimate.
- Under ~1,000 words unless the topic genuinely needs more.
- One or two screenshots, code transcripts, or diagrams if they help.

## What Still Applies

- No banned vocabulary. No em dashes.
- No feigning surprise. No relentless enthusiasm.
- Active voice. Plain verbs.
- Vary sentence length.

## What Relaxes

- First person is generous, not overly careful.
- Parentheticals and asides used more freely.
- "It turns out…" and "Huh, that's odd" are allowed.
- Tone is curious and informal, not measured and composed.
